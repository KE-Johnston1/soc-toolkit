# Phase 1 Hypothesis Tracking

Phase 1 connects the multi-source case to explicit competing hypotheses. The hypothesis layer records which observations support a hypothesis and keeps the result separate from analyst assessment.

For `CASE-MULTI-001`, the investigation considers legitimate administration, credential attack or automated authentication abuse, possible account compromise after successful authentication, misconfigured or automated service activity, and unknown or novel activity.

Repeated authentication failures, a following successful login, and cross-source correlation are supporting observations only. They do not establish attribution, malicious intent, compromise, or incident status.

A hypothesis can be supported, challenged, unresolved, or remain open. Supporting evidence is not proof, and challenging evidence should remain visible rather than discarded.
