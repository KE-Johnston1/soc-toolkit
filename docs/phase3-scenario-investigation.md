# Phase 3 Scenario Investigation Guide

Phase 3 turns the decision framework into reusable analyst case studies. Each case deliberately contains enough evidence to form competing explanations but preserves uncertainty where the evidence does not establish the answer.

## Analyst reporting standard

Every case report should answer:

1. What was observed and when?
2. Which evidence sources corroborate or challenge the observation?
3. What competing hypotheses remain plausible?
4. What is the current assessment and confidence?
5. What risk or impact is established versus potential?
6. Does the case meet escalation criteria, and why?
7. What response is authorised and safe?
8. What evidence is still missing?
9. What would be required for closure?
10. What should change in detection, telemetry, playbooks, or training?

## Scenario coverage

| Case | Investigation context | Deliberate uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| CASE-PHISH-001 | Phishing / executive impersonation | Sender compromise and user impact are unverified |
| CASE-NET-001 | C2 / potential exfiltration | Destination ownership, process attribution and data type are unknown |
| CASE-INSIDER-001 | Privileged / insider-context activity | Maintenance context conflicts with unusual source baseline |

The scenarios are synthetic. They demonstrate analyst reasoning rather than operational attack procedures.
